Back Day

BUILD A WIDER, THICKER BACK

Lats (Width)

Lat Pulldown

Target: Lats and Back Width

How to do it:

  • Sit with thighs secured under the pads.
  • Grip the bar wider than shoulder-width.
  • Pull the bar down to your upper chest, elbows down.
  • Return slowly until arms are fully stretched.

Pull-Ups

Target: Lats and Upper Back

How to do it:

  • Hang from the bar with palms facing away.
  • Pull your chest up toward the bar.
  • Keep your core tight and avoid swinging.
  • Lower slowly to a full hang.

Straight-Arm Pulldown

Target: Lat Isolation

How to do it:

  • Stand facing a high cable with a bar or rope.
  • Keep arms nearly straight and chest up.
  • Sweep the bar down to your thighs.
  • Return slowly while feeling the lats stretch.

Mid Back (Thickness)

Barbell Bent-Over Row

Target: Mid Back Mass and Strength

How to do it:

  • Hinge forward at the hips with a flat back.
  • Grip the bar just outside your knees.
  • Pull the bar to your lower ribs.
  • Lower with control without rounding your back.

Seated Cable Row

Target: Mid Back Thickness

How to do it:

  • Sit tall with knees slightly bent.
  • Pull the handle to your stomach.
  • Squeeze your shoulder blades together.
  • Extend arms slowly without leaning too far.

One-Arm Dumbbell Row

Target: Lats and Mid Back

How to do it:

  • Place one hand and knee on a flat bench.
  • Hold a dumbbell with your other arm hanging down.
  • Pull the dumbbell toward your hip.
  • Lower slowly and repeat on both sides.

Lower Back & Traps

Deadlift

Target: Lower Back, Glutes and Full Back

How to do it:

  • Stand with the bar over your mid-foot.
  • Grip the bar and keep your back flat.
  • Drive through your feet and stand tall.
  • Lower the bar under control along your legs.

Back Extension

Target: Lower Back Strength

How to do it:

  • Secure your feet and hips on the bench.
  • Cross your arms over your chest.
  • Lower your torso with a straight spine.
  • Raise back up until your body is in a line.

Dumbbell Shrugs

Target: Upper Traps

How to do it:

  • Stand holding dumbbells at your sides.
  • Lift your shoulders straight up toward your ears.
  • Pause and squeeze at the top.
  • Lower slowly without rolling the shoulders.
