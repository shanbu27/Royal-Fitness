The Fat Shredder

About 30 Min • HIIT • Intermediate

Intermediate

Warm-up (5 min): easy jog or jumping jacks, arm circles, leg swings.

Circuit: 40 seconds work, 20 seconds rest. Do all 5 moves, then repeat for 3 rounds (rest 1 minute between rounds).

  1. Burpees
  2. High knees
  3. Mountain climbers
  4. Jump squats
  5. Skater hops

Cool-down (5 min): slow walk and stretch calves, quads, hamstrings.

Easier: swap burpees for step-back burpees and jump squats for normal squats.

Marathon Base

60 Min • LISS • Beginner

Beginner

Warm-up (5 min): brisk walk, then a few gentle leg swings.

Main set (50 min): jog 4 minutes, walk 1 minute, repeat 10 times. Stay at a pace where you can still talk (Zone 2).

Cool-down (5 min): slow walk and light stretching.

Too hard? Jog 2 minutes and walk 2 minutes. As you improve, shorten the walks until you can jog the full 50 minutes.

The Sprint Finisher

15 Min • Sprints • Advanced

Advanced

Warm-up (4 min): easy jog, high knees, butt kicks, two 10-second build-up runs.

Main set: sprint 20 seconds as fast as safely possible, then walk 90 seconds. Repeat 6 times.

Cool-down: slow walk until your breathing is normal.

Sprint on a flat, even surface with good shoes. Stop if you feel a sharp pain or dizziness. Use this once a week at most.

SMART TIPS

Hydrate

Drink water before, during and after. In hot weather, drink more.

Warm Up Always

5 minutes of easy movement prepares your heart, joints and muscles.

Progress Slowly

Add no more than 10% to your time or distance each week to avoid injury.

Recover

Do hard HIIT or sprints no more than 3 days a week, with easy days between.

This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice. If you have a heart condition, chest pain, dizziness or any health concern, speak to a doctor before starting hard cardio.
