Shoulder Day

BUILD STRONGER SHOULDERS

Front Delts

Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press

Target: Front and Side Delts Mass

How to do it:

  • Sit on a bench with back support.
  • Hold dumbbells at shoulder height.
  • Press overhead until arms are extended.
  • Lower slowly back to shoulder level.

Barbell Overhead Press

Target: Shoulder Strength

How to do it:

  • Hold the bar at collarbone height.
  • Brace your core and squeeze your glutes.
  • Press the bar straight overhead.
  • Lower it back to your shoulders with control.

Dumbbell Front Raise

Target: Front Delt Isolation

How to do it:

  • Stand holding dumbbells in front of your thighs.
  • Raise one or both arms to shoulder height.
  • Keep a slight bend in the elbows.
  • Lower slowly without swinging.

Side Delts

Dumbbell Lateral Raise

Target: Side Delts Width

How to do it:

  • Stand with dumbbells at your sides.
  • Raise arms out to the sides to shoulder height.
  • Lead with your elbows, not your hands.
  • Lower slowly and keep the weight light.

Cable Lateral Raise

Target: Side Delt Constant Tension

How to do it:

  • Stand sideways to a low cable pulley.
  • Grab the handle with the far hand.
  • Raise your arm out to shoulder height.
  • Lower slowly against the cable.

Upright Row

Target: Side Delts and Traps

How to do it:

  • Hold a bar with a shoulder-width grip.
  • Pull it up toward your chest, elbows high.
  • Stop at about chest height.
  • Lower slowly under control.

Rear Delts

Reverse Pec Deck Fly

Target: Rear Delts

How to do it:

  • Sit facing the machine pad.
  • Hold the handles with arms straight.
  • Pull your arms back and out to the sides.
  • Return slowly without letting the weight drop.

Face Pull

Target: Rear Delts and Upper Back

How to do it:

  • Set a rope on a cable at face height.
  • Pull the rope toward your face.
  • Spread the ends apart, elbows high.
  • Return slowly to a full stretch.

Bent-Over Rear Delt Fly

Target: Rear Delt Isolation

How to do it:

  • Hinge forward with a flat back.
  • Hold dumbbells hanging below your chest.
  • Raise arms out to the sides.
  • Squeeze at the top and lower slowly.
