Leg & Core Day

BUILD STRONG LEGS AND A POWERFUL CORE

Quads

Barbell Back Squat

Target: Quads, Glutes and Overall Leg Mass

How to do it:

  • Place the bar on your upper back and stand shoulder-width apart.
  • Brace your core and push your hips back and down.
  • Lower until thighs are at least parallel to the floor.
  • Drive through your feet to stand up.

Leg Press

Target: Quads and Glutes

How to do it:

  • Sit with your back flat against the seat.
  • Place feet shoulder-width apart on the platform.
  • Lower the platform until knees reach about 90 degrees.
  • Press up without locking your knees.

Leg Extension

Target: Quad Isolation

How to do it:

  • Sit with the pad resting on your lower shins.
  • Extend your legs until they are straight.
  • Squeeze your quads at the top.
  • Lower slowly under control.

Hamstrings & Glutes

Romanian Deadlift

Target: Hamstrings and Glutes

How to do it:

  • Hold a bar or dumbbells in front of your thighs.
  • Push your hips back with a slight knee bend.
  • Lower until you feel a hamstring stretch.
  • Drive your hips forward to stand tall.

Lying Leg Curl

Target: Hamstring Isolation

How to do it:

  • Lie face down with the pad above your heels.
  • Curl your heels toward your glutes.
  • Squeeze at the top.
  • Lower slowly without lifting your hips.

Walking Lunges

Target: Glutes, Quads and Balance

How to do it:

  • Stand tall holding dumbbells at your sides.
  • Step forward and lower until both knees are bent 90 degrees.
  • Push through your front heel to step forward.
  • Alternate legs with each step.

Calves

Standing Calf Raise

Target: Calves (Gastrocnemius)

How to do it:

  • Stand with the balls of your feet on a raised edge.
  • Lower your heels for a full stretch.
  • Rise up as high as you can on your toes.
  • Pause at the top and lower slowly.

Seated Calf Raise

Target: Calves (Soleus)

How to do it:

  • Sit with the pads resting on your lower thighs.
  • Lower your heels as far as comfortable.
  • Push up onto your toes.
  • Pause and lower slowly.

Bodyweight Squat

Target: Legs and Beginner Strength

How to do it:

  • Stand with feet shoulder-width apart.
  • Extend your arms forward for balance.
  • Sit your hips back and down, keeping your chest up.
  • Stand back up by pushing through your heels.

Core (Abs & Obliques)

Plank

Target: Core Stability

How to do it:

  • Rest on your forearms and toes.
  • Keep your body in a straight line from head to heels.
  • Squeeze your abs and glutes.
  • Hold for 20-60 seconds without sagging.

Hanging Knee Raise

Target: Lower Abs

How to do it:

  • Hang from a bar with arms straight.
  • Lift your knees toward your chest.
  • Curl your pelvis up at the top.
  • Lower slowly without swinging.

Russian Twist

Target: Obliques

How to do it:

  • Sit with knees bent and lean back slightly.
  • Hold your hands together or a light weight.
  • Rotate your torso side to side.
  • Keep your chest up and move with control.

Cable Crunch

Target: Upper Abs

How to do it:

  • Kneel under a high cable holding a rope by your head.
  • Curl your ribs toward your hips.
  • Squeeze your abs at the bottom.
  • Return slowly to the start.
