Chest Day

BUILD A STRONGER CHEST

Upper Chest

Incline Dumbbell Press

Target: Upper Chest Mass and Strength

How to do it:

  • Sit on an incline bench set to 30-45 degrees.
  • Hold dumbbells at chest level with palms facing forward.
  • Press upward until arms are extended.
  • Lower slowly and in a controlled motion.

Low-to-High Cable Fly

Target: Upper Chest Isolation

How to do it:

  • Stand between two low cable pulleys.
  • Grasp handles with palms facing forward.
  • Bring hands together upward toward eye level.
  • Maintain a slight bend in the elbows throughout.

Incline Barbell Bench Press

Target: Upper Chest Mass and Strength

How to do it:

  • Lie on an incline bench under the barbell.
  • Grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder-width.
  • Lower the bar smoothly to your upper chest.
  • Press the bar up explosively while keeping feet planted.

Middle Chest

Barbell Bench Press

Target: Middle Chest Overall Mass

How to do it:

  • Lie flat on the bench with feet firmly planted.
  • Grip bar slightly wider than shoulder-width.
  • Lower bar slowly to mid-chest.
  • Press upwards until arms are locked out.

Flat Dumbbell Fly

Target: Middle Chest Stretch & Definition

How to do it:

  • Lie on a flat bench holding dumbbells above chest.
  • Lower weights out to sides with slight elbow bend.
  • Squeeze chest to bring dumbbells back together.
  • Control the weight slowly on the descent.

Push-Ups

Target: Middle Chest & Core Stability

How to do it:

  • Place hands shoulder-width apart on the floor.
  • Keep body straight from head to heels.
  • Lower chest until almost touching the ground.
  • Push body back up to starting position.

Lower Chest

High-to-Low Cable Fly

Target: Lower Chest Definition

How to do it:

  • Attach pulleys to the highest setting.
  • Pull handles downward and together in front of waist.
  • Pause and squeeze lower chest at the bottom.
  • Return slowly to starting height.

Chest Dips

Target: Lower Chest & Triceps

How to do it:

  • Grasp parallel bars and support your body weight.
  • Lean torso forward slightly to target chest.
  • Lower body until elbows are bent at 90 degrees.
  • Push back up to full arm extension.

Decline Dumbbell Press

Target: Lower Chest Mass

How to do it:

  • Secure feet at end of decline bench and lie back.
  • Hold dumbbells at chest level.
  • Press dumbbells upward over lower chest.
  • Lower controlled back to starting position.
