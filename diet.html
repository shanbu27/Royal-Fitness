THE MACROS

Three nutrients drive your results. Here is what each one does and how much you need.

Proteins

1.6 - 2.2 g / kg

Repairs and builds muscle. Spread it across 3-5 meals, about 25-40 g each.

Eggs, chicken, fish, beef, lentils, yogurt, whey

Carbs

3 - 6 g / kg

The fuel for heavy sessions. Eat more on training days, especially before and after.

Oats, rice, potatoes, teff injera, bread, fruit

Fats

20 - 30% of calories

Supports hormones, joints and brain health. Keep fats mostly unsaturated.

Nuts, avocado, olive oil, eggs, fatty fish, seeds

LOCAL FOOD SWAPS

You do not need expensive supplements. Everyday local foods work great.

Shiro & Misir (Lentils)

Good plant protein and fiber. Use less oil, and pair with injera for carbs.

Teff Injera

A solid carb source. Eat it around training, and watch the portion if cutting.

Eggs

Cheap, complete protein. 3-4 boiled eggs a day fit almost any plan.

Lean Tibs & Fish

Choose lean cuts, ask for less oil, and add vegetables on the side.

Milk & Yogurt (Ergo)

An easy way to add protein and calories, great for building muscle.

Fasting Days

On fasting days use shiro, lentils, chickpeas and beans as your protein.

DAILY HABITS

Hydrate

Drink 2.5-3.5 litres of water a day, more when it is hot or you train hard.

Sleep 7-9 Hours

Muscle grows while you rest. Poor sleep raises hunger and slows recovery.

Eat Around Training

Have carbs and protein 1-2 hours before, and a protein meal within 2 hours after.

Be Consistent

Track your weight weekly. Change calories by 100-200 only if progress stalls for 2-3 weeks.
