Triceps Day

BUILD POWERFUL TRICEPS

Long Head (Overhead)

Overhead Dumbbell Extension

Target: Triceps Long Head

How to do it:

  • Hold one dumbbell with both hands overhead.
  • Lower it behind your head, elbows pointing up.
  • Keep your upper arms still.
  • Extend back up until arms are straight.

Overhead Cable Extension

Target: Triceps Long Head Stretch

How to do it:

  • Face away from a high cable with a rope.
  • Hold the rope behind your head.
  • Extend your arms forward and up.
  • Return slowly to a full stretch.

Skull Crushers

Target: Triceps Mass

How to do it:

  • Lie on a flat bench holding an EZ bar overhead.
  • Bend your elbows to lower the bar toward your forehead.
  • Keep your upper arms still.
  • Extend back up to the start.

Lateral & Medial Heads

Rope Pushdown

Target: Triceps Lateral Head

How to do it:

  • Stand facing a high cable with a rope.
  • Keep your elbows tight to your sides.
  • Push down and spread the rope at the bottom.
  • Return slowly to chest height.

Straight Bar Pushdown

Target: Triceps Overall Mass

How to do it:

  • Grip a straight bar at chest height.
  • Keep your upper arms still.
  • Push the bar down until arms are straight.
  • Return slowly without leaning forward.

Triceps Kickback

Target: Triceps Isolation

How to do it:

  • Hinge forward with your upper arm parallel to the floor.
  • Hold a dumbbell with your elbow bent 90 degrees.
  • Extend your arm straight back.
  • Squeeze and return slowly.

Compound Triceps

Close-Grip Bench Press

Target: Triceps Strength and Mass

How to do it:

  • Lie flat and grip the bar shoulder-width.
  • Lower the bar to your lower chest.
  • Keep elbows close to your body.
  • Press up until arms are locked out.

Bench Dips

Target: Triceps for Beginners

How to do it:

  • Place hands on a bench behind you.
  • Extend your legs forward.
  • Lower your body until elbows reach 90 degrees.
  • Push back up to straight arms.

Diamond Push-Ups

Target: Triceps and Inner Chest

How to do it:

  • Form a diamond with your hands under your chest.
  • Keep your body in a straight line.
  • Lower your chest to your hands.
  • Push back up to full extension.
