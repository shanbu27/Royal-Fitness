Biceps Day

BUILD BIGGER, STRONGER ARMS

Long Head (Peak)

Incline Dumbbell Curl

Target: Biceps Long Head Stretch

How to do it:

  • Sit back on a 45-60 degree incline bench.
  • Let your arms hang straight down.
  • Curl the dumbbells up without moving your elbows.
  • Lower slowly to a full stretch.

Barbell Curl

Target: Overall Biceps Mass

How to do it:

  • Stand holding a bar with palms facing forward.
  • Keep elbows close to your sides.
  • Curl the bar up to shoulder height.
  • Lower under control without swinging.

Dumbbell Curl

Target: Biceps Size and Balance

How to do it:

  • Stand with dumbbells at your sides.
  • Curl one arm up while rotating the palm upward.
  • Squeeze at the top.
  • Lower slowly and alternate arms.

Short Head (Width)

Preacher Curl

Target: Biceps Short Head

How to do it:

  • Rest your upper arms flat on the preacher pad.
  • Curl the weight up toward your shoulders.
  • Pause and squeeze at the top.
  • Lower slowly until arms are almost straight.

Concentration Curl

Target: Biceps Peak and Isolation

How to do it:

  • Sit and brace your elbow against your inner thigh.
  • Curl the dumbbell up toward your shoulder.
  • Squeeze hard at the top.
  • Lower slowly and repeat on both sides.

Cable Curl

Target: Constant Biceps Tension

How to do it:

  • Stand facing a low cable with a straight bar.
  • Keep elbows pinned to your sides.
  • Curl the bar up to chest height.
  • Lower slowly against the cable.

Brachialis & Forearms

Hammer Curl

Target: Brachialis and Forearms

How to do it:

  • Hold dumbbells with palms facing each other.
  • Curl up without turning your wrists.
  • Keep your elbows by your sides.
  • Lower slowly to the start.

Reverse Barbell Curl

Target: Forearms and Brachialis

How to do it:

  • Hold a bar with an overhand grip.
  • Keep your wrists straight.
  • Curl the bar up to shoulder height.
  • Lower slowly with control.

Zottman Curl

Target: Biceps and Forearm Strength

How to do it:

  • Curl the dumbbells up with palms facing up.
  • Rotate your palms to face down at the top.
  • Lower slowly in the overhand grip.
  • Rotate back to palms up at the bottom.
